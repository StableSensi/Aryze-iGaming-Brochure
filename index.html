01The problem

Deposits are lost
at the cashier.

Not to broken technology. To friction, at the last step, after you already paid to bring the player there.

Won the player.
Lost the deposit.

Marketing, bonuses and affiliates buy the visit. Whether the deposit lands is decided in the final thirty seconds — in the cashier. Declines and chargebacks come on top, and cost you separately.

Every failed payment is money already spent.

Affiliates, campaigns, paid traffic. It all lands in the cashier, and that is the one place still built on card forms.

01The problem

Five problems.
The ones that cost most.

There are more. These are the five that come up in every operator conversation we have.

Fees grow with the deposit

Chargebacks and declines

The worst chargeback exposure of any vertical, and limits keep tightening.

A rail goes down, cards pick up the bill

The usual fallback is cards, at card prices, on your worst day.

Too many providers

Every PSP adds a contract, an integration and a reconciliation by hand.

FX eats the margin

Money between markets moves through correspondents, at their spreads.

05For your developers

One call to the API.

Authenticate, initiate, receive the webhook. Our quickstart is timed at about five minutes.

# base url
https://payment-api.openfinance.aryze.io

# initiate a payment
curl -X POST /v1/payments/initiate \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…" \
  -d '{ "amount": 100.50, "currencyCode": "EUR" }'

# response
{ "flowUrl": "https://payment.aryze.io/...", "status": "PENDING" }
